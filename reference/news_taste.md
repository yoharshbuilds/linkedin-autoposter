# Rahul Mathur's news instinct (learned from his last 70 posts)

This is the filter for "is this a Rahul story, and what is the angle." Used to score and rank
candidate news, and to brief the writer.

## His beats (rough weight by frequency)
1. **VC fund returns / exit / IPO math** (high). Decodes the REAL multiple from RHP/filings when the
   media reports a misleading number. Blume 36.65x on Turtlemint, Sauce VC 8x on Innovist.
2. **Indian consumer / D2C brand teardowns** (high). Revenue trajectory, margins, who is winning.
   Zara India stalling, SNITCH ₹11Cr->₹900Cr, Yes Madam, Agilitas, Trent/Zudio.
3. **Unit economics "let me do the math"** (high). Recompute cost-per-order, gross margin, take rate
   from public P&L. Urban Company support cost, Yes Madam gross margins.
4. **AI in the enterprise / agents** (medium-high). Practical adoption, founder-led AI, agent limits.
   Bajaj Finance FinAI, Nugget AI, Emergent, "agents fail in production".
5. **Founder spotlights, often from events** (medium). Repeat founders, IIT-Bombay deeptech, demo days
   (MTW 2026). Ignosis, Terrastack, Mavs AI, PawPath.
6. **Funding rounds / acquisitions / celebrity cap tables** (medium). Always framed with a lesson,
   never a bare announcement. Yes Madam ₹50cr, Kohli/Anushka into Agilitas.
7. **Structural India theses** (medium). "China built Super Apps, India builds Super Companies",
   buy-vs-build, Korea/global comparisons.
8. **Personal / human** (Titan watch, family). NOT automatable. Skip these.

## What makes something post-worthy to him (newsworthiness triggers)
- Recent + **India-centric** business event with **decodable numbers** (₹ crore, x, %, XIRR, CAGR).
- A **non-obvious angle**: "the media missed a nuance", "the real number is X not Y", a myth to bust.
- Public, citable source: **RHP/DRHP filings, TRACXN, AppBrain, company P&L, roadshow disclosures,
  MCA financials**. He shows his work.
- A **lesson** for founders or investors falls out of it.
- Sectors he loves: consumer/D2C, fintech/BFSI, SaaS/AI, VC mechanics. Cool on pure macro/politics.

## What he avoids
- Generic press-release reposts with no added analysis.
- Pure global news with no India angle.
- Hype with no numbers to verify.

## Scoring rubric (0-10) for a candidate news item
- +3 India-centric business/startup event
- +3 has concrete numbers to decode (funding amount, valuation, revenue, multiple, margin)
- +2 there is a non-obvious angle or a number the media is likely getting wrong
- +1 in a favored sector (consumer/D2C, fintech/BFSI, SaaS/AI, VC)
- +1 a clear founder/investor lesson exists
- -3 no India angle / no numbers / pure announcement
Pick items scoring >= 7. For each, the output should include the angle he would take and which
image template fits (returns_table / bar_chart / app_showdown / calc_worksheet / thesis_card / photo / none).
