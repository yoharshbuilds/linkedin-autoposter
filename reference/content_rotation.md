# Rotate content + creative like Rahul (do not repeat the same lane)

Rahul does NOT only post funding/returns. He rotates across content types, and each type
has a creative that fits. Pick a DIFFERENT content type and creative from the last 2 posts.

## Content types (rotate across these)
1. vc_returns        - fund exit / MOIC / cap-table math on an IPO or secondary.        -> returns_table
2. funding_round     - a notable raise, but only with a sharp angle (multiple, step-up). -> calc_worksheet / thesis_card
3. unit_economics    - "show the math": implied per-order cost, margin, CAC, savings.    -> calc_worksheet
4. financials_growth - revenue / PAT trajectory over years.                              -> bar_chart
5. product_app       - app or product comparison, ratings, downloads.                    -> app_showdown
6. ai_thesis         - a take on AI / vertical AI / what is actually changing.           -> thesis_card / photo
7. founder_operator  - a lesson, a hiring view, a building-in-public reflection.         -> thesis_card / photo
8. market_contrarian - a contrarian read on a sector or a "the coverage missed X" take.  -> thesis_card / returns_table
9. ipo_exit          - DRHP / listing math, who made what.                               -> returns_table
10. shutdown_failure - why a startup died, the post-mortem math.                          -> calc_worksheet / thesis_card
11. milestone_people - congrats, a notable hire/move, ecosystem moment (uses real photo). -> photo

## Rotation rule (enforced)
- Do NOT use the same content_type as either of the last 2 posts.
- Do NOT use the same image template as the last post.
- Prefer the type that best fits the freshest strong story, but break ties toward variety.
- Funding stories are fine, just not back-to-back. If the last post was funding, pick a
  non-funding angle next (unit economics, an AI thesis, a product take, an operator lesson).

## Tone variety (not every post is analytical)
He mixes hard-numbers breakdowns with shorter opinion/observation posts and the occasional
warm/people post. Vary the format: sometimes the 3-point breakdown, sometimes a tight
contrarian take, sometimes a single sharp observation. Always no em dashes.
